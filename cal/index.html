Three Calendars

Tap a button on your iPhone and Apple Calendar will offer to subscribe. It then refreshes on its own, so nothing here ever needs you to open an app again.

Anchors

The fixed points. parkrun every Saturday at 07:30, your mum every Wednesday at 14:30, and a one-off nudge to text Kelvin that you can delete once it is sent.

Times are deliberately floating, which means 07:30 stays 07:30 in Sheffield. A routine that survives travel is the entire point.

If the buttons do not work

Some browsers block the webcal:// scheme. In that case, in Calendar go to File → New Calendar Subscription on a Mac, or Settings → Apps → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar on an iPhone, and paste:

Two things worth knowing

Read-only
A subscribed calendar cannot be edited on your phone. That is a feature rather than a limitation — these are things that get decided once and then stop being negotiable at eight in the morning.
Updates
They refresh roughly twice a day. When something changes, it is regenerated with python3 cal/build.py and every subscriber picks it up without doing anything.
