Twenty-Two Hours

You will not want to write this in late October. You will have just launched an event, flown three continents in a fortnight, and you will be tired. So here it is now, with the arithmetic done and the objections answered.

The argument

The objection is not about your lifestyle. It is “you would be less available.” Own the number rather than dodging it, then change what it is measured in.

29hunder this proposal, summer

A reduction of six to eight hours depending on the season, or 17 to 23 per cent. Say the number before they work it out.

On those two days you keep the whole Maltese morning

Malta sits an hour or two ahead of the UK, which works in your favour here. A 12:00–19:00 Singapore day is 04:00–11:00 UTC, and that catches 09:00 to 13:00 in Malta through the summer, and 09:00 to 12:00 in winter.

So you are not asking to disappear for most of their day. You are keeping the half where priorities get set, and releasing the afternoon.

And the hours you release are your worst

What moves is 11:00–15:00 UTC — Malta's afternoon. In Singapore that is 19:00 to 23:00: hours eleven to fifteen of your waking day.

The trade is not eight hours for nothing. It is four tired hours twice a week, for a whole Maltese morning and a functioning person. Everyone in that room knows what hour fifteen feels like.

Say what covers the gap

Do not leave it as an absence. Written input in advance for anything scheduled in the 11:00–15:00 UTC window on those two days, plus one named escalation route for genuine emergencies. An availability objection dies faster against a stated alternative than against reassurance.

The clocks change on 25 October

Europe leaves summer time on Sunday 25 October — two days after NEXTPredict ends, and the same week you plan to have this conversation. Singapore has no daylight saving, so the relationship between your day and HQ's moves by an hour twice a year and you absorb it silently.

Which raises a question you should answer before writing the rate in: are your hours fixed in Singapore terms, or do they follow Malta? If they follow Malta, your winter shift is an hour later than your summer one — finishing at midnight rather than eleven — and you have been giving that hour away every winter without it ever being a stated term. Either way, put it in writing this time. which is it?

Do not mention the COO

You corrected me that there is no Colombia team — it is your COO going off-grid on visits. So that comparison is out of the document, and it should stay out of your mouth.

Two reasons. It is a dig at a senior colleague, which costs you more than it wins. And a negotiation where one checkable claim turns out to be wrong is a negotiation where every other claim gets checked. Every number you bring should survive somebody looking it up.

Two things to know before you walk in

You have less leverage than the case deserves. One client funds your salary, which holds your pass, which is your permission to live in the same country as your partner. Walking away is not a move available to you until there is a second client. So negotiate like somebody who wants a long relationship, because you do.

And more leverage than you feel. You launched their event. Replacing you in January, four days after a successful first edition, is a genuinely bad idea and they know it. Those two facts are both true at once.
